# Phase 11 report: classic action gauntlet

Phase 11 expands the endless run from two to three worlds. Level 3 is an industrial hazard course with a red/yellow global multicolor palette, warning-panel skyline rhythm, warning-stripe platforms and a new reachable high-tier route. The cycle is now L1 → L2 boss → L3 factory → `SYSTEM OK`, then returns to a harder Level 1 without resetting rank, score or remaining lives.

The factory adds two distinct non-solid floor hazards: triangular spikes and zigzag electrical fields. Both use authoritative `HAZARD` flags, fixed player-foot probes and bright background-2 ink for stronger contrast. Three overhead warning lanes announce a reusable falling-material sprite; its 50-frame warning phase has no visible sprite or collision box, and after every impact cycle it moves to the next marked lane. A separate ball enters from the right, rolls left through the ground corridor and gains another movement step at higher danger ranks. Neither action hazard can be removed by stomping or shooting.

The Level-2 boss now begins a projectile pattern after all items are collected. It alternates two energy-orb heights, shortens its reload at high rank and accelerates again in its low-HP rage phase. The orb reserves hardware sprite 4 only after the corresponding 1-Up object has disappeared, preserving the eight-sprite contract without a multiplexer. Hits use a fixed software AABB independent of the orb artwork.

The post-Phase-11 playability pass replaces the boss body's per-frame player tracking with an autonomous bounded arena patrol. Early encounters move at a readable half rate; rank and low-HP rage increase pace without turning the boss back into a magnetic follower. Losing the final life now enters a nine-second `CONTINUE` countdown. Fire restarts the current section with three lives while preserving score and danger rank; expiry leads to the separate `GAME OVER` new-game state.

The combat follow-up replaces the boss's broad 72-pixel projectile-hit acceptance with a 28x17-pixel central weak core. A blind floor-height shot now misses. Fire remains the horizontal hand shot; Fire+Up throws a high ballistic bomb and suppresses jumping, while Fire+Down throws a descending bomb suitable for attacking from an upper tier. Both bombs share the one-projectile/cooldown limit and use dedicated pointer `$49` artwork. Static spike and electric hazards now fill their complete 16-pixel metatile height with much larger bright warning shapes.

A collision follow-up separates the expanded boss body from the platform above it: the player's 21-pixel body must overlap the boss's 42-pixel body, and only a descending entry through its upper eight pixels is a stomp. The six-pixel gap at the authored upper platform therefore cannot drain boss HP. Small enemies use a directed 24x21 projectile box, restoring hits across their complete width while retaining the boss's separate Level-2 weak core.

The third layout originally increased the reversible delta table to 148 seven-byte records; the current readability revision uses 144. A single-frame application exceeded the safe PAL budget, so Phase 11 introduces `GAME_LOAD_LAYOUT`: at most 16 records update the authoritative map and expanded character world per loading frame before Screen A/B are rebuilt. Steady gameplay, scrolling and Color RAM behavior remain unchanged.

The L03R02 tuning pass replaces nine isolated, hard-to-read floor traps with three two-metatile warning fields separated by long safe runs. Falling-object lanes retain at least two clear floor columns of visual buffer. Spike and electrical glyphs no longer use the green floor ink: in the factory palette their large silhouettes are exclusively black, red and yellow, making danger readable before committing to a jump.

Validation covers all three maps, objective support, the Level-3 approach step, paired spike/electric fields, safe falling-lane buffers, both bomb modes, the boss-core miss/hit pair, autonomous boss patrol, boss-orb damage, Continue acceptance/timeout, Level-3 completion and the three-rank endless restart. `make build test` and the 7,680-frame PAL soak pass. `build/l03r02-readable-traps.png` was inspected for unmistakable grouped hazards, safe floor contrast, the factory palette, action sprites, status split, stable platforms and VIC artifacts.
